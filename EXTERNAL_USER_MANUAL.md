## 1. Creating Your Account

Access to the Clinical Research Management System is invitation-based to ensure security and proper authorization. You will receive access to a study through an invitation sent by your site administrator or site point of contact. This section explains how to create your account and begin working with your assigned studies.

### Receiving Your Invitation

When a site administrator or point of contact adds you to a study, you will receive an email invitation to join the system. The invitation email will include:

- A brief introduction to the system
- The name of the person who invited you
- The study or studies you've been assigned to
- A secure link to create your account

**Example email:**

```
Subject: Invitation to Join Clinical Research IO

Hi [Your Name],

You have been invited by [Administrator Name] to join CRIO (Internal Only) 
as an external user on the following study/studies:

Additional eCourse Training

To accept this invitation and join, please click on the link below:

[Secure Account Creation Link]

Cheers,
The ClinicalResearch.IO Team
```

### Creating Your Account

Follow these steps to create your account after receiving the invitation:

1. **Open the invitation email** from Clinical Research IO in your email inbox.

2. **Click the secure link** provided in the email. This link will direct you to the account creation page.

3. **Complete the account creation form** by entering the following information:
   - **Username**: Choose a unique username for your account
   - **Password**: Create a secure password that meets the system requirements
   - **First Name**: Enter your legal first name
   - **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name
   - **Email**: Verify your email address (this may be pre-filled)

4. **Review the Privacy Policy** by clicking the link provided at the bottom of the form.

5. **Click "SIGN UP"** to complete your account creation.

6. **Verify your email address** if prompted. You may receive a verification email to confirm your account.

7. **Log in to the system** using your newly created username and password.

### Important Information for Existing Users

**Note**: If you already have a CRIO account from a previous study invitation, you will **not** receive a new invitation email when added to an additional study. Instead, simply log in to your existing account using your current credentials. The new study will automatically appear in your dashboard under your assigned studies. You can access all studies associated with your account from a single login.

### Account Security Requirements

Your password must meet the following security requirements:

- Minimum of 8 characters
- At least one uppercase letter
- At least one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one special character (e.g., @, #, $, %)

### Troubleshooting Account Creation

If you encounter any issues during account creation:

**The invitation link has expired:**
- Contact your site administrator to request a new invitation link
- Invitation links are typically valid for 7 days

**You forgot your password:**
- Click "Already have an account? Login" at the bottom of the signup form
- Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page
- Follow the password reset instructions sent to your email

**You're already registered:**
- Click "Already have an account? Login" at the bottom of the signup form
- Log in with your existing credentials
- Contact your site administrator if you don't see the expected study

**Technical difficulties:**
- Ensure you're using a supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)
- Clear your browser cache and cookies
- Try accessing the link in a private/incognito browser window
- Contact your site administrator or technical support for assistance
